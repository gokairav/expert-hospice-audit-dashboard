Privacy Policy -- Expert Hospice Audit Assistant

Last updated September 2026.

Expert Hospice Audit Assistant ("the extension") is an internal tool built for Expert Hospice staff to generate and submit chart-audit records to the ATTAbot! audit console. It is not distributed to the general public and is not intended for use outside Expert Hospice.

What the extension does

The extension runs in your Chrome browser. It:

  • Builds a text prompt describing an audit checklist, which you paste into Claude for Chrome
  • Accepts the resulting report back from you and parses it into structured fields
  • Sends that structured data, along with your login session, to the Expert Hospice ATTAbot! Supabase backend (the only external service the extension talks to)

What data is involved

Audit content you submit may include patient-identifying information and clinical details drawn from the patient's chart (name, MRN, diagnosis, clinical findings) as part of the audit record itself. This data is transmitted only to the Expert Hospice-operated Supabase backend, over HTTPS, and is never sent to any other third party by the extension. The extension does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind.

Where data is stored

Submitted audits, findings, and account information (name, email, role) are stored in Expert Hospice's private Supabase project, access-controlled by row-level security so only authenticated Expert Hospice staff accounts can read or write records. The extension itself stores only your login session token locally in your browser (via chrome.storage.local) so you stay signed in -- no patient data is stored in the browser itself.

Who can access this data

Only Expert Hospice staff accounts explicitly created by an Expert Hospice administrator can sign in and access audit data, gated by role (auditor, reviewer, admin, viewer).

Data retention and deletion

Audit records are retained as part of Expert Hospice's compliance/QAPI documentation. Contact your Expert Hospice administrator with any data correction or retention questions.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the extension's data handling can be directed to your Expert Hospice administrator.
